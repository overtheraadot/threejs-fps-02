BREACHLINE — a close-quarters tactical FPS in three.js: five rounds, first to three, in the Annex 9 facility. Breach the core hall with squadmate VEX, clear four hostiles that patrol, hold cover, flank and defuse, or plant the charge on the DATA CORE and hold it till it blows. Between rounds, spend the pay in the buy phase: an AR-77 rifle, an M590 shotgun, frag grenades and Kevlar. Hitscan gunplay with headshots, recoil and spread that punish firing on the move, aim-down-sights, crouching and cover, a live minimap, and a kill feed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click to breach; WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, 1/2/3 to switch weapons, G for a frag grenade, hold E to plant the charge, B opens the buy menu between rounds.
